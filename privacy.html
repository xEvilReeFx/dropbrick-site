Privacy policy

Updated 29 October 2026

DropBrick is an app for tracking your LEGO® collection and finding deals. This page explains what personal data it uses, why, and what your rights are. We’ve tried to keep it short and plain. The rules for using the app are in the Terms of use.

Who is responsible

DropBrick is an independent project by Oriol Baltrons Fabregas, Catalonia (Spain), who is the data controller. Contact: support@dropbrick.app.

What we don’t do: we don’t sell your data, show ads, use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools, and we don’t make decisions about you by automated means or build profiles. The app doesn’t ask for your location, camera, contacts or photos, and the purchase links to shops carry nothing that identifies you.

Who else handles your data

  • Supabase — database and sign-in. Your data is stored in the EU (Ireland).
  • Resend — sends our emails (confirmation, password reset). It sees your email address and the message.
  • Expo, and Apple or Google — deliver push notifications.
  • The shops — when you tap a purchase link you leave DropBrick. The shop may set cookies and process data under its own policies. DropBrick doesn’t receive information that identifies you.
  • GitHub and Cloudflare — host this website, manage its domain and forward the support@ mailbox.
  • Brickset — the set data comes from Brickset.com, which we download to our own server (it never sees your account). The set photos, though, load straight from its image server (images.brickset.com), so that server sees your IP address and basic device details whenever the app shows a photo. No account data is sent to it.

Transfers outside the EU

Your account data is stored in the EU. Some of the providers above, such as Resend, Expo, Google, GitHub or Cloudflare, may process data outside the EU/EEA; they do so under safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or adequacy frameworks.

How long we keep it

While your account exists. When you delete your account (Profile → Delete account), your account and everything linked to it — collection, lists, settings, notification token — is erased. The notification history is deleted automatically after 60 days. A notification token is removed when its device stops answering, and we keep at most your 20 most recent devices. Emails you sent to support are kept only as long as needed to answer you and can be deleted if you ask. Our providers may keep technical logs for a limited time under their own policies, and emails already sent stay in your own mailbox.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a portable copy, and you can object to its use or withdraw your consent at any time. You can delete your account yourself from the app. For anything else write to support@dropbrick.app; we answer within one month. You can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the AEPD (aepd.es).

Children

DropBrick is for people aged 14 or over, and you confirm it when you create your account. If we learn that an account belongs to someone younger, we delete it.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. Each account can only read its own data (row-level security) and passwords are stored hashed. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect so there is less to protect.

This website

This website doesn’t use cookies or analytics, and loads nothing from third parties: its typefaces and scripts are served from the site itself. The password-reset page keeps nothing in your browser: the sign-in session lives only in memory while the page is open.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way we’ll update the date above and, when it affects how your data is used, tell you in the app.

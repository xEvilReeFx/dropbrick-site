Privacy policy

Updated 8 October 2026

DropBrick is an app for tracking your LEGO® collection and finding deals. This page explains what personal data it uses, why, and what your rights are. We’ve tried to keep it short and plain.

Who is responsible

DropBrick is an independent project by Oriol Baltrons Fabregas, Catalonia (Spain), who is the data controller. Contact: support@dropbrick.app.

What we collect, and why

DataWhat forLegal basis
Account: email address, password (kept only as a one-way hash by our sign-in provider; we never see it), username, and optionally your first and last nameCreate and secure your account, let you sign in, and send you the emails about it (confirmation, password reset).Contract (providing the service)

What we don’t do: we don’t sell your data, show ads, use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools. The app doesn’t ask for your location, camera, contacts or photos, and the purchase links to shops carry nothing that identifies you.

Who else handles your data

  • Supabase — database and sign-in. Your data is stored in the EU (Ireland).
  • Resend — sends our emails (confirmation, password reset). It sees your email address and the message.
  • Expo, and Apple or Google — deliver push notifications.
  • Brickset and Rebrickable — the app asks their catalogues for set information directly, so they see your IP address and what you search for, but not your account.
  • Awin and the shops — when you tap a purchase link you leave DropBrick. The affiliate network and the shop may set cookies and process data under their own policies. DropBrick earns a commission if you buy; it doesn’t receive information that identifies you.
  • GitHub and Cloudflare — host this website, manage its domain and forward the support@ mailbox.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr — this website loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, and the password-reset page loads a script from jsDelivr; they see your IP address.

Transfers outside the EU

Your account data is stored in the EU. Some of the providers above, such as Resend, Expo, Google, GitHub or Cloudflare, may process data outside the EU/EEA; they do so under safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or adequacy frameworks.

How long we keep it

While your account exists. When you delete your account (Profile → Delete account), your account and everything linked to it — collection, lists, settings, notification token and suggestions — is erased. Our providers may keep technical logs for a limited time under their own policies, and emails already sent stay in your own mailbox.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a portable copy, and you can object to its use or withdraw your consent at any time. You can delete your account yourself from the app. For anything else write to support@dropbrick.app; we answer within one month. You can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the AEPD (aepd.es).

Children

DropBrick isn’t meant for children under 14. If we learn that an account belongs to one, we delete it.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. Each account can only read its own data (row-level security) and passwords are stored hashed. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect so there is less to protect.

This website

This website doesn’t use cookies or analytics. The password-reset page keeps nothing in your browser: the sign-in session lives only in memory while the page is open.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way we’ll update the date above and, when it affects how your data is used, tell you in the app.
